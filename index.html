Hi, I am Fernanda

I spent 12 years in branding before I started asking myself some inconvenient questions about what I actually wanted my life to look like. One thing led to another: I studied Health Coaching, trained as a Pilates instructor — mat, reformer, pre- and post-natal — and couldn't stop asking people what their sign was, so of course I studied Astrology too.

I personally have many passions that I am putting together to help women figure out who they are, what works for THEM, how they want to move and what changes they want in their lives — if they want any change. Because we only live once and I am convinced that we should enjoy more, stress a little bit less, learn from situations, keep growing, but always ALWAYS stay true to ourselves, not to what is expected from us. Aligned decisions help you expand without guilt, drama or what-ifs.

In short, I help you build a life you are proud of, so you don't need to run away from it.
