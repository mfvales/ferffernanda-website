Hi, I'm Fernanda.

Certified Reformer & Mat Pilates instructor and Integrative Health Coach in Dubai — and, for over a decade before this, a brand strategist. Today I help women move better, think clearer, and choose braver.

hi, it's me

I didn't start on a reformer.

I spent 12+ years in corporate marketing and brand strategy, across Argentina, Europe and the Middle East. But the tell was already there: while I was still building other people's brands, I trained as an Integrative Nutrition Health Coach. Deep down, I was already drafting the corporate exit — I just didn't know it yet.

When redundancy came, it wasn't the end of the plot — it was the push I'd been waiting for. I went all in: added Reformer and Mat Pilates to the coaching, and built a practice around the thing that had always pulled me back to myself. Today I teach across Dubai's studios, coach privately, and still put my marketing brain to work on freelance brand projects. Two worlds, one throughline — helping women feel good in their bodies and honest about their lives.
